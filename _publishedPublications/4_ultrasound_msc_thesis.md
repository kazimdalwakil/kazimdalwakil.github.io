# description: A deep learning pipeline combining deformable multi-angle registration, transformer-based cross-angle fusion, and a joint dual-task decoder for ultrasound reconstruction and segmentation. Evaluated on 42 hand and forearm acquisitions, the model achieves PSNR of 40.5966 dB, SSIM of 0.9592, Dice of 0.9273, and IoU of 0.8652 on a held-out subject-level test set. A four-way ablation study with Wilcoxon signed-rank testing confirms statistically significant contributions from both registration and fusion, with large effect sizes on reconstruction metrics (Cohen's d up to 13.8). A custom composite reconstruction loss addresses ultrasound's sparse, non-uniform signal structure.
