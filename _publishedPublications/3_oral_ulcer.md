# description: CompAttNet is a lightweight dual-head CNN (4.79M params, 0.493 GFLOPs) for multi-task oral disease diagnosis, built on EfficientNetV2-B0 with a novel AttentionHub-v2 module combining sequential Triplet and Squeeze-and-Excitation attention. An 8-configuration ablation study reveals a role-complementarity principle — spatial and channel attention modules outperform two spatial modules combined. The model achieves 99.51% subtype accuracy and 99.06% binary accuracy across 7 oral disease classes, outperforming 9 CNN and Transformer baselines while remaining 2.1× smaller than EfficientNetV2-B2. Grad-CAM++ and LIME analyses confirm clinically relevant lesion-focused predictions.
